Let me tell you a story about a book dragon..

## Hi :D

Nice to meet you!

&nbsp;

### A bit of the story
A long time ago I worked as a consultant, advising on self development trainings to everyone I knew and making a buck out of it. So, as I did the courses, I was reading a lot of self-development books... like... a ton. My bookshelves appeared to contain that only genre (*read about 7 out of 50 something*).

Switching to digital marketing, having the self development mindset I had a lot of digital marketing books as well (*read 3, left about 6 in the middle, all out of 20 something*)

When I truly found God back in 2017 the Bible was great nourishment, and still is, but sometimes the goal of reading the entire Bible seems so far as it is kinda hard to soak the maximum of just the one book :/

So I got like *20 something books* that were related to several christian topics like how to read the bible, devotionals, prayers, household, fasting ... still on my way to finish *my 4th book*.

As I made my way into cybersecurity, I started reading more technical books only related to cyber (*read 1 out of 10*). And I'm counting that one book as a win as it got me a certification ^^

&nbsp;

### Reality check
So.... if my math is right, *from 100-ish books I've read about 15*.

In sum, I like to buy a lot of books but most of them either I start reading them and don't even finish or I simply don't open them. Making me a book dragon, buying way more than what I read!

&nbsp;

### What I needed
I don't know if you're anything like me but, if you are, fear not! It's totally fine to be like that. Although, if you let your thoughts ramble too much, at some point you will see yourself a little bit on the dark side thinking "why can't I just finish one book?" for a long time.

It took me a while but what I needed was: **the right genre!**

For me, fantasy does the trick.

And if they are related to **dragons**, I'm usually all in!

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

I already had some fantasy books but, of course - as a decent book dragon - as soon as I read 3 in a row my next move was to rush to the nearest library and buy another 20 fantasy books xD

&nbsp;

***My top 3:***
  1. Iron Flame (Fourth Wing)
  2. Fourth Wing
  3. The hitchhiker's guide to the galaxy
