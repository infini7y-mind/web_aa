Nowadays it might be shocking but....

**I will always choose my wife over my children!**

## Why?!

One day kids will leave, that's just life!

When they do, if I don't take care of my marriage, I will end up with a total stranger in the house.

Besides...

I hope God continues to be merciful and don't take away any child I have.

But if He does... who will be able to get me more children?

Answer is... **MY WIFE!!!!**

## Some more whys for you :)

Who knows me better than anyone? --> MY WIFE!

Who reminds me of my compromises? --> MY WIFE!

Who makes the best accountability partner? --> MY WIFE!

Who will endure my pain with better care than anyone else? --> MY WIFE!

Who translates my needs to other people when I'm not able? --> MY WIFE!

&nbsp;

### Who gets me happiness?
Well, you already know my asnwer ;) and this one is particularly important, and deserves its own chapter!

&nbsp;

Ok, but... Why?

Easy to understand, not that easy to put into practice.

Answer is 'Cause we married to make the other happy, not because "I want to be happy!!!", "I DESERVE to be happy!!! So make me happy! I don't have to do anything for you"

In my opinion, most modern marriages fail because they are self centered or unilateral!

If both marry to make the other happy, both will put in the effort to make sure the other is happy, and in the end... both will be happy!

&nbsp;

But not everything will be easy!

Two different people, two different brains, two different perspectives of the world!

Both have to fight for it, both have to work for it, both have to want it to work.

For me and my wife the only way is through communication.

Are we *always* able to communicate? **No!**

**But we try!!** And if during the day we couldn't make it, at night we try again, and tomorrow we try to be even just a tiny bit slightly better. Even if it's like 0,0000000000000001%

&nbsp;

Some common advice we've been giving whenever we see couples in trouble:
- Talk about what you want (dreams, hobbies, wishes,) and align expectations
- Speak up when you don't like something (remember: one is not on the other's brain)
- Pray at night together (get stronger with God, together!)
- Don't EVER sleep angry (your bed is a sanctuary!!!)

&nbsp;

Don't let the world say that family is wrong when it's actually a blessing!

Cherish your wife AND your children, and a few years from now, down the road, you will be able to look at your life and see how rewarding the journey was and still is.
